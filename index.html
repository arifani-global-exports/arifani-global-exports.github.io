Who we are

A sourcing partner built around clarity and execution.

Arifani Exports is a Kenya-based sourcing and trade-support business focused on helping buyers identify suitable suppliers, understand product requirements and coordinate the steps toward a workable transaction.

We aim to make cross-border sourcing more practical: clear specifications, responsive communication and transparent coordination from first enquiry to shipment planning.

What we do

Practical support for serious buyers.

Start with one clear requirement. We help define the brief, identify sourcing routes and coordinate next steps. Scope, availability and fees are confirmed per assignment.

01 / Sourcing

Supplier sourcing

Research and shortlist potential Kenyan or regional suppliers against your product, volume, destination and commercial criteria.

02 / Due diligence

Supplier checks

Coordinate business-document checks, reference requests and evidence collection. Verification scope and findings are shared transparently.

03 / Trade support

Export coordination

Support specification alignment, document follow-up, packaging discussions and freight or port coordination with relevant providers.
