Why We Do It

Smallholders grow it. We make sure it lands.

Most export failures aren't agricultural, they're administrative: a missing certificate, a misdeclared HS code, a cold chain broken at customs. We built Arifani Investments Exports to close that gap for growers and buyers who don't have a compliance department of their own.

Our Mission

Move fresh and raw agricultural goods across borders without the shipment ever becoming the weak link in the trade.

Our Vision

A trade lane where a grower in Naivasha and a buyer in Rotterdam trust the same paperwork, the same cold chain, the same word.

How A Shipment Moves

Four stages, from the field to your customs desk.

01 — Harvest

Grade at source

Product is graded and logged within hours of harvest or distillation, at the farm or press.

02 — Quality Control

Lab & visual check

Batch testing, moisture and purity checks, and phytosanitary inspection before packing.

03 — Documentation

Manifest & certs

HS classification, certificates of origin, and customs paperwork prepared ahead of pickup.

04 — Freight

Air, ocean, or road

Booked against your Incoterm of choice, tracked door-to-door until proof of delivery.
